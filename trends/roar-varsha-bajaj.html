Roar by Varsha Bajaj

A May 2026 children’s novel you can buy on Bookshop. The store page already calls it a national bestseller, so the stage is Mid.

Where to buy

Identified on October 28, 2026 (2026-10-28)

Signals are not guarantees. A sell-out, a search spike or a celebrity sighting can fade. This page describes public reporting. It is not a forecast, and it is not a reason to buy anything as an investment.

Mid

What it is

Roar is a middle-grade novel by Varsha Bajaj, published by Nancy Paulsen Books on May 12, 2026. ISBN 9780593699058. The story sends a boy to a tiger sanctuary in India. The Bookshop.org page leads with “national bestseller” and notes that Bajaj is already a New York Times bestselling author. We are repeating the bookstore’s label, not a chart we pulled ourselves.

Signals we can actually cite

  • A place to buy: the same hardcover is on Amazon, which is the Where to buy button. Bookshop still lists it, and that source link has no affiliate tag.
  • How far along it is: that same page’s bestseller line. A book already wearing that label is past “very early.”

We do not have a BookTok view count, and we are not treating the bestseller line as a number of copies sold.

Why this stage and meter

Mid, meter 2/5. The useful public fact is that a 2026 children’s title is easy to order and is already being sold as a bestseller. That is distribution, not a fresh spike.

signal 2/5
