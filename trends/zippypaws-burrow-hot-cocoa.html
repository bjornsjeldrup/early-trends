ZippyPaws Burrow Hot Cocoa

A ZippyPaws burrow shaped like a cocoa mug, sold on Amazon with three squeaky marshmallows. The Jigglerz pickle did not have an Amazon product page we could verify.

Where to buy

Identified on October 28, 2026 (2026-10-28)

Signals are not guarantees. A sell-out, a search spike or a celebrity sighting can fade. This page describes public reporting. It is not a forecast, and it is not a reason to buy anything as an investment.

Early–mid

What it is

The Amazon listing is ZippyPaws Burrow Hot Cocoa: a plush mug and three small marshmallow toys. The dog is meant to pull the marshmallows out. The page listed it around $18, with about 385 ratings. The Jigglerz Pickle, which the brand shop tags as new, did not have a product page on Amazon we could open, so it is not the link.

Signals we can actually cite

  • The brand’s format: ZippyPaws’ shop is built around burrow toys. This is one of those, with a checkout.
  • Ratings: about 385 on the Amazon page. That is repeat buying, not a counted sell-out.

Why this stage and meter

Early–mid, meter 2/5. The burrow idea is established. This mug is a specific one you can buy. We are not calling the pickle’s “trending” badge a fact about this toy.
