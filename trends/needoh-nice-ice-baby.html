Signals are not guarantees. A sell-out, a search spike or a celebrity sighting can fade. This page describes public reporting. It is not a forecast, and it is not a reason to buy anything as an investment.

Already mainstream – not an early pick. We first listed this as “Early” on November 4, 2026. A reader on Reddit pointed out that NeeDoh had been hyped on TikTok, Facebook and Instagram since before Easter 2026, and that the Aldi Halloween drop drew lines before opening. The reader was right. We were roughly nine to ten months late.

What went wrong

We listed this while a restock streak was still going. The timeline below shows the craze was already national by then: a shortage, news coverage and store lines had all happened. This page stays up as the write-up of that miss.

First-signal timeline

  1. June 2025

    Schylling reports that NeeDoh demand doubled the company’s yearly sales between 2024 and 2025. NeeDoh — About

  2. Fall 2025

    A NeeDoh Advent calendar sells out and the first viral buzz arrives before the holidays. Boston Globe

  3. January 2026

    A parent’s TikTok about the Nice Cube sets off a wave of copycat videos. (Boston Globe)

  4. February 2026

    Schylling sells six to eight months of inventory in about six weeks; stores run “drops” with long lines. The Strategist

  5. April 2026

    National coverage of the shortage, resellers and knock-offs. Business Insider · The Atlantic

  6. June 2026

    Retailer orders backordered to early fall; Schylling rations stock. (The Strategist)

  7. October 7, 2026

    Aldi’s Halloween NeeDoh drop: lines before opening, sold out in minutes, limit four. USA Today · Parade

  8. November 4, 2026

    We published this page as “Early”. About ten months after the first clear mass-market signal. That was a miss.

What it is

The Amazon listing is Schylling NeeDoh Nice Ice Baby: one 1.25-inch cube, pack of one, color may vary. The photo shows a translucent blue cube in the box, with pink and purple cubes beside it. It is a sensory fidget for ages 3 and up, the teenie size of the brand’s Nice Cube. The listing had about 5,044 ratings when we wrote this. Amazon is the only shop link on this page.

Needoh ice cube fidget toy

A needoh ice cube is a squishy ice cube fidget: one small cube you squeeze in a hand, not a set and not a kitchen toy. Shoppers also type needoh fidget toy, schylling needoh nice cube, or squishy ice cube fidget for kids. Nice Ice Baby is the name on the box. Those shorter phrases are how people look it up.

Signals we can actually cite

  • Amazon restocks: Restockd tracked about 83 Amazon restocks in 30 days for Nice Ice Baby, with the latest around November 3–4, 2026. That is a scarcity pattern. It is not a promise the cube stays sold out, and it is not a forecast that it will go viral.
  • Restock alerts: TYPA’s NeeDoh tracker flags Amazon back-in-stock alerts, including this cube. The same page follows the wider NeeDoh line, which restocks heavily too. An alert app’s own totals are not a sales forecast.
  • What the toy is: a wholesome fidget and sensory cube, age 3 and up. No adult theme, and nothing to do with drugs.

Stage, trend age and meter

Stage: Mainstream – not an early pick. First clear mass-market signal: January 2026, about ten months before we published. The 4/5 meter reflected how scarce the cube was when we wrote this.
