What it is

Brick is a phone blocker sold. People use it to make a phone harder to pick up — a physical or app-linked friction, depending on the setup — alongside dumb phones and other blockers. We are covering it as a habit tool in a shopping category. That is the whole claim. See current price on Amazon.

Signals we can actually cite

  • A reported wave, not a clinical result: Hindustan Times, Fortune, and a 2026 tech-trends roundup all describe people trying to use their phones less, with Brick named among app blockers and analog habits. Fortune’s piece groups it with dumb phones and social-media limits.
  • What we will not say: we make no health claim. Brick is a habit tool. If a source frames heavy phone use in medical language, that wording is theirs, not a result we are reporting.

Not a health product. If you need help with phone use that is hurting you, talk to a person who is qualified to give it. This page is a trend note about a gadget.

Why this stage and meter

The sources agree that a phone-free wave is underway and that blockers, including Brick, are part of how people are acting on it. That is media and cultural coverage, not proof of effect. No download total, no clinical result, and no sell-out figure appears in the way we are willing to quote. Meter: 2/5. Stage: mid, because the behavior is already a reported wave rather than a single prototype.
