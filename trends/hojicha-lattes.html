Hojicha lattes

Roasted green-tea lattes are showing up on US menus after a sharp search lift. Early-to-mid: the flavor has a paper trail, not a sold-out story.

Where to buy

Identified on October 28, 2026 (2026-10-28)

Signals are not guarantees. A sell-out, a search spike or a celebrity sighting can fade. This page describes public reporting. It is not a forecast, and it is not a reason to buy anything as an investment.

Early–mid

What it is

Hojicha is roasted green tea. The trend version is the latte: iced on café boards, and at home as a powder. The Coffee Bean & Tea Leaf has put hojicha drinks on a fall menu. Black Sheep Coffee has launched a hojicha range on a US autumn menu. The at-home name we are pairing with those menus is Hojicha Co. powder. We do not have a merchant link for that powder in this source list.

Signals we can actually cite

  • Search: Martin Bauer cites “hojicha latte” searches up 173%. A Google Trends write-up puts interest at an all-time high in March 2026 and calls out “hojicha vs matcha” as a breakout query. Breakout means a sharp rise from a low base, not a headcount of drinkers.
  • Menus, not sell-outs: two US coffee chains have published hojicha drinks for autumn. That is distribution. It is not a report that cups sold out.

We are not comparing hojicha to matcha as a health choice. This is a flavor and menu trend only.

Why this stage and meter

Search is the signal we can point at: a reported 173% lift for “hojicha latte,” an all-time search high in March 2026, and a breakout for “hojicha vs matcha.” US menu launches show the drink leaving search and landing on boards. They are not sell-out figures, so they do not add a resale point. Short-video counts are not in these sources. Meter: 2/5. Nothing here is a nutrition claim.
