Summer Fridays Lip Butter Balm

A vanilla lip balm that already has an Allure seal on the tube. Not a secret. It replaces Rhode’s Peptide Lip Shape, which did not have an exact Amazon match.

Where to buy

Identified on October 28, 2026 (2026-10-28)

Signals are not guarantees. A sell-out, a search spike or a celebrity sighting can fade. This page describes public reporting. It is not a forecast, and it is not a reason to buy anything as an investment.

Mid

What it is

Lip Butter Balm in Vanilla is a Summer Fridays lip mask in a squeeze tube. The Amazon listing we opened is sold under that brand name, at about $24, and shows on the order of 11,045 ratings. The photo is the vanilla tube. It carries a printed Allure Best of Beauty 2024 seal. Rhode’s Peptide Lip Shape stays off this list: the Amazon pages under Rhode’s name were the Peptide Lip Tint, a different product.

Signals we can actually cite

  • A printed award: the seal on the tube in the product photo says Allure Best of Beauty 2024. That is on the package, not a number we audited.
  • Retail depth: about 11,045 ratings on the Amazon listing. A count that large is why the stage is Mid, not very early.

Why this stage and meter

Mid, meter 3/5. An award mark plus a large rating count is adoption, not a fresh sell-out we timed. It is here because it is the same kind of lip product people restock, and because it has a real Amazon page.

signal 3/5
