What it is

The Pupsicle, from Woof, is a refillable toy that holds a frozen treat for dogs. The thing to watch beside the toy is the refill, because a gadget that needs a consumable is a different business from a one-time chew.

Signals we can actually cite

  • Search: a 2026 pet-trends roundup is the source for search lifts around this kind of product. We are not inventing a percentage the roundup does not state as a Pupsicle statistic.
  • Short-video commerce: a TikTok Shop reviews page is up for the Pupsicle. A reviews page is evidence the listing exists and is being discussed. It is not a sales total.

Why this stage and meter

Search lifts are cited at the pet-trend level, and the product has both a brand page and a TikTok Shop reviews page, which is a short-video commerce footprint. Neither source gives a sell-out, a rank, or a percentage we can honestly repeat. Meter: 2/5. Stage is “Mid” because this is an established refill product, not a first prototype.
