Our Place Wonder Oven

Our Place’s compact countertop oven, now a normal Amazon listing. Fellow’s Opus 2 is a grinder, and the Ode on Amazon is not that grinder.

Where to buy

Identified on October 28, 2026 (2026-10-28)

Signals are not guarantees. A sell-out, a search spike or a celebrity sighting can fade. This page describes public reporting. It is not a forecast, and it is not a reason to buy anything as an investment.

Mid

What it is

The Amazon listing is the Our Place Wonder Oven in the Spice color: a compact countertop oven that air fries, bakes, roasts, toasts, broils, and reheats. The photo is that oven. Fellow’s Opus 2 conical grinder did not have an Amazon page for that model. The Fellow grinder that does appear is the Ode, which is a different machine, so Opus 2 is replaced.

Signals we can actually cite

  • A named oven, on Amazon: the product page spells out the six jobs and the included rack, basket, and pan.
  • How far along: Wonder Oven has been a talked-about countertop appliance since it launched. We do not have a fresh sell-out count for this color, so the stage stays Mid.

Why this stage and meter

Mid, meter 2/5. The interesting part is that a design-led oven people already know is a straightforward Amazon listing, not that it is a secret.
