Crocs Getaway Platform Toe Loop

A platform toe-loop sandal Crocs itself badges as TikTok Viral, listed at $44.99. The rating count is Crocs’ figure, not one we measured.

Where to buy

Identified on October 28, 2026 (2026-10-28)

Signals are not guarantees. A sell-out, a search spike or a celebrity sighting can fade. This page describes public reporting. It is not a forecast, and it is not a reason to buy anything as an investment.

Early–mid

What it is

The Getaway Platform Toe Loop is a Crocs sandal, SKU 210834. The product page we opened lists the price at $44.99 and puts a “TikTok Viral” snipe on the gallery. The same product record shows 619 ratings with a 4.5 average. Those numbers belong to Crocs’ page.

Signals we can actually cite

  • Short video, as labeled by the retailer: the badge is on this SKU’s page, not only in a site-wide menu.
  • Reviews on the same page: 619 ratings. That is a stock of reviews, not a view count and not a sell-out.

A separate TikTok Shop landing page throws around a much larger sold figure for a similarly named sandal. We are not using that number. It is not Crocs’ product page, and we did not audit it.

Why this stage and meter

Three signals are thin but real: the retailer’s own viral badge, a review count on that page, and a live checkout. There is no independent search chart and no resale premium in what we are willing to cite, so the meter stays at 3/5 and the stage stays Early–mid. Crocs as a brand is not a secret. This SKU is the part that still carries a trend tag.
