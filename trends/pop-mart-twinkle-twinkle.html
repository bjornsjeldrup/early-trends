Pop Mart Twinkle Twinkle

A new Pop Mart character line with a hot resale print in China and a US series that only just went on sale. Early in the West, further along in China.

Published October 28, 2026

Signals are not guarantees. A sell-out, a search spike or a celebrity sighting can fade. This page describes public reporting. It is not a forecast, and it is not a reason to buy anything as an investment.

Early in the West

What it is

Twinkle Twinkle is a Pop Mart character line — plush pendants and blind-box figures — arriving as coverage asks what the company sells if Labubu cools. In the US, the Live the Ordinary series launched on 1 October 2026.

Signals we can actually cite

  • Sell-outs: plush pendants have been reported selling out within minutes.
  • Resale: a Valentine hidden figure priced at ¥89 was reported at ¥699 on POIZON, about 6.8 times retail. That is one marketplace print, not a promise the gap holds.
  • A new US shelf: Live the Ordinary is on Pop Mart’s US site from 1 October 2026. We are not calling that US launch a sell-out.
  • Context, not a signal by itself: reporting on Labubu cooling is why a next character matters to Pop Mart. It does not prove Twinkle Twinkle will repeat that arc.

Caveat: the same resale conversation includes a reported month-on-month dip. Premiums can shrink as fast as they appear. We do not suggest buying toys to flip.

Why this stage and meter

The meter is illustrative, not a score. Sell-outs and resale above retail are clearly reported, so the resale signal is on. We are not awarding points for short-video counts or search charts, because those are not in the sources below. A reported month-on-month dip in resale is why this stays at 2/5 even though the China print looks hot. Stage label: early in the West, mid in China.
