Stages we use

Every pick is dated from the first clear sign it was taking off, and labelled. Early means we caught it soon after the first signals. Rising means it is clearly growing, but no longer brand new. Mainstream – not an early pick means we got there too late, and we say so. Some cards still say Early–mid or Mid for a pick that is moving but not brand new.

Signals, in brief

We watch a mix of search, social, community and retail signals, weighted for the category. The approach is in How we spot trends.
