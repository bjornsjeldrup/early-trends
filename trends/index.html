Stages we use

StageWhat it usually looks like
Very earlyA few creators or fan communities mention it; little or no search interest yet.
EarlyShort-form videos repeating; search interest lifting from a low base; first moves on Amazon Movers & Shakers or crowdfunding.
RisingMainstream mentions, restocks selling quickly, resale listings above retail.
Peak / pastEverywhere, widely copied. Covered here only as a case study.

Signals differ by category

Resale prices matter a lot for collectibles but barely at all for kitchen gadgets; BookTok and library holds matter for books; Kickstarter and Product Hunt matter for tech. See the signals-by-category table or pick a category.
