Saie SuperSuede Baked Eyeshadow

Saie’s baked eyeshadow singles launched August 18, 2026. The shade people chased next is Bellini, a rose-gold shimmer co-created with Hannah Chody, which sold out within hours on October 15.

Where to buy

Identified on November 10, 2026 (2026-11-10)

Signals are not guarantees. A sell-out, a search spike or a celebrity sighting can fade. This page describes public reporting. It is not a forecast, and it is not a reason to buy anything as an investment.

Early

What it is

Saie SuperSuede Baked Eyeshadow is a single compact of baked powder eyeshadow. WWD reported that the line opened on August 18, 2026 with 11 neutral shades at $24, in store and online at Sephora and on saiehello.com. Six shades were demi-matte and five were low-lit shimmer. Bellini is a later shade: a rose gold shimmer eyeshadow single co-created with TikTok creator Hannah Chody. Saie’s product page calls the formula a talc free baked eyeshadow. That is the brand’s description, not a wear test we ran.

Hannah Chody Bellini eyeshadow

Saie Bellini eyeshadow is the shade in the photo. Cosmetics Daily and Glasswend describe it as the peachy, rose-gold shimmer Chody developed with Saie while looking for a replacement for discontinued Tom Ford Beauty Golden Peach. People who followed that search also type tom ford golden peach eyeshadow dupe. The articles call Bellini a shade in this line, not a Tom Ford product.

Signals we can actually cite

  • Line launch, August 18, 2026: WWD’s August 10 story sets the on-sale date, the $24 price, the 11 opening shades, and Sephora plus Saie. Saie’s own waitlist note says, “See just how easy eyeshadow can be on August 18th.”
  • Bellini sell-out, October 15, 2026: Cosmetics Daily says the shade sold out within hours of that launch and that Saie became one of the most-searched brands on Sephora that day. Glasswend says it sold out in hours and calls Saie the most-searched brand on Sephora that day. Allure says the sell-out in a few hours is according to Business of Fashion, and that Sephora confirmed Saie was among the top-searched brands that day.
  • TikTok Shop record, not confirmed: Glasswend says an exclusive TikTok Shop gift set allegedly set a sales record and that the company did not confirm it. Allure, citing Business of Fashion, reports a similar claim and says a TikTok representative declined to comment.
  • Restock churn: trackalacker shows the Saie listing for Bellini flipping in and out of stock from October 21 to October 27, 2026, at $24, and out of stock on the checks after 8:10 UTC on October 27. The page still said Out of Stock when we opened it on November 10. Saie’s product page listed Bellini as unavailable that same day.
  • Amazon: the line is on Amazon US at $24, marked “New on Amazon in past month,” with 6 global ratings (4.8 out of 5) and a Best Sellers Rank around #14,094 in Beauty & Personal Care. That is not a top-100 long run.

Why this stage and meter

Early, meter 3/5. Trend age is about 2.8 months on November 10, 2026. We count from August 18, 2026, the line launch, because that date is earlier than the October 15 Bellini sell-out. Under three months is the Early label. If it is still growing, this label turns Rising after November 18, 2026. The sell-out, the Sephora search note, and a short restock churn are the signals. We do not have a unit count, and the TikTok Shop record was not confirmed by the brand or by TikTok.
