What it is

InvisOutlet Aura, from San Jose company Intecular, replaces a standard US wall outlet. Two receptacles can be switched separately. The faceplate has a strip of color light Intecular sells as a night light and as a status glow (a door left open, laundry finished). It speaks Matter, so the listing names Apple Home, Alexa, Google Home, SmartThings and Home Assistant. It needs a neutral wire. If swapping an outlet is not a job you already do, this is not an impulse buy. We are not writing the install.

We file it under Home & Decor because the thing you see is light on a wall, in a hallway or a bedroom. It is also a gadget.

Signals we can actually cite

  • Crowdfund speed, 19 August 2025: Intecular’s launch post says the Kickstarter opened that day. A company LinkedIn post says it fully funded in 22 minutes and passed $100,000 within a week. The finished total, on Intecular’s site and on LaunchBoom, is $157,110 from 868 backers, 1,571% of the goal. An earlier product, InvisOutlet Pro, had its own tally (the launch post says over $160,000 from 839 backers). That number is not this campaign.
  • After the campaign: Matter Alpha, 4 March 2026, reported the first Amazon sale after a campaign it described as more than $157,000. On 31 October 2026 Intecular’s own shop still said out of stock, with shipping expected by the end of October 2026. The same day Amazon had ASIN B0G63TQFV7 in stock, sold by Intecular, shipped by Amazon, with about 24 ratings at 4.6. See current price on Amazon.
  • A mild visual, not a five-second wow: the colored strip reads in a clip. It does not explain a trick the way a scent pod does. What we can cite is the 22-minute fund, a real checkout, and a brand shop that had not caught up.

Why this stage and meter

Early–mid, meter 3/5. The campaign is a year old and Amazon has been up since early 2026, but 24 ratings is not a household product, and the company’s own store was still quoting a ship date on the day we identified it.
