Early–mid

What it is

The Yoto Mini 4th Gen is a portable audio player for children. Yoto’s US page lists it. A card or the green side button starts the audio. The company describes it as screen-free listening and says this generation adds a programmable Green Button, more storage, and an IP67 dust and water rating. “Screen-free” here is the product’s own description. This page does not claim the player improves development, sleep, or learning. See current price on Amazon.

Signals we can actually cite

  • Launch: Yoto’s US press note is dated September 10, 2026. Pre-orders opened that day, with Mini dispatch estimated from September 30. The note prices the Mini and the larger Player. See current price on Amazon.
  • How known the brand already is: a founders’ note on Yoto Space says the company has reached over six million children. That is the company’s number, which is why the stage is not “very early.”

Why this stage and meter

Early–mid, meter 2/5. The hardware is weeks old. The brand is not. We do not have an independent count of how many Minis shipped after September 30.
