Method · explainer

How we spot trends early

Most viral products leave a trail long before they go mainstream. Our job is to find that trail while it’s still faint.

What we changed (Nov 2026). After reader feedback, we tightened our filter and relabelled late picks as Mainstream. Those pages stay at the same addresses, with the label and the dated first signal. Misses we write up live on the Case studies shelf.

What we watch

We track a broad mix of public signals: what people search for, what creators make videos about, what niche communities talk about, how fast stock moves at retailers, and how new launches are doing. No single signal tells the whole story. A trend only shows up when several of them start moving together.

How we weigh them

Signals don’t count equally, and how much each one counts depends on the category. A beauty trend leaves a different trail than a gadget or a toy. We give the most weight to how fast interest is growing, not how big it already is. We weigh early signals higher than late ones, and genuine community interest higher than marketing. On top of that we apply our own scoring model and editorial judgement, refined pick by pick. That’s the part we keep to ourselves.

The mainstream filter

Before a product can become a pick, it has to pass a filter that weeds out things that have already peaked. If everyone already knows about it, it isn’t an early trend, however popular it is.

Trend age

Every pick is dated from the first clear sign that it was taking off, and gets a label:

  • Early. Caught soon after the first signals.
  • Rising. Clearly growing, but no longer brand new.
  • Mainstream – not an early pick. We got there too late. We say so openly.

Learning from misses

We review every pick at regular intervals afterwards and keep score of hits and misses. When we get it wrong, we write it up, and the model gets better each time. Those write-ups live on the Case studies shelf.

What this is not

Signals are clues, not guarantees. Nothing here predicts future results. Many products show strong early signs and still fade.

What we won’t cover

Anything adult, drug- or vape-related, unsafe for kids, or built on hype schemes.
