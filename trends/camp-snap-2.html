Camp Snap 2

The second Camp Snap, a screen-free point-and-shoot at $69.99. The page says the first ten batches sold out. That sentence is the maker’s, and we did not count the units.

Where to buy

Identified on October 28, 2026 (2026-10-28)

Signals are not guarantees. A sell-out, a search spike or a celebrity sighting can fade. This page describes public reporting. It is not a forecast, and it is not a reason to buy anything as an investment.

Early

What it is

Camp Snap 2 is a screen-free digital camera. The brand shop lists it at $69.99 and the variant we checked was available. The page describes six on-camera filters, a lock meant for younger photographers, and USB-C charging. It also says batches 1 through 10 sold out, that the company makes small runs, and that orders ship first come, first served.

Signals we can actually cite

  • Sell-out, in the company’s words: “Batches 1–10 Sold Out.” We did not see a warehouse report. The sentence is on the product page.
  • Retail: the same page still takes an order at $69.99, so “sold out” here means those batches, not that the product is impossible to buy.

The first Camp Snap already had a life as a screen-free camera. This pick is the second model, not a claim that nobody has heard of the brand.

Why this stage and meter

Early, meter 2/5. One cited sell-out line from the maker, plus a live price. No independent chart.
