Loop Quiet 2

Reusable earplugs for noise, travel, focus, and sleep, $24.95. WIRED’s March 2026 guide still picks them. The review counts say this is Mid. Not a medical device write-up.

Signals are not guarantees. A sell-out, a search spike or a celebrity sighting can fade. This page describes public reporting. It is not a forecast, and it is not a reason to buy anything as an investment.

Mid

What it is

Loop Quiet 2 is a pair of reusable silicone earplugs. The US shop lists them at $24.95, and the product was available when we checked. The brand describes them for focus, travel, and sleep, and the page lists 14 dB NRR. Target also has a Quiet 2 page. This is a comfort and noise product. It is not a treatment, and nothing here is medical advice.

Signals we can actually cite

  • Press: WIRED’s earplug guide, updated March 30, 2026, names Quiet 2 as a top pick for sleep and says the pair is made to sit flush so side-sleeping is easier. That guide prints 24 dB. The US shop page prints 14 dB NRR. Those are different labels. This page does not try to turn one into the other.
  • How far along: Loop’s own compare table on the Quiet 2 page shows 13,190 reviews for Quiet 2. Target’s listing showed 2,138 reviews. Those are the retailers’ counts. They are why the stage is Mid.

Why this stage and meter

Mid, meter 3/5. A 2026 guide, two retailers, and review totals that are already large. The early part is only that Quiet 2 is the newer revision WIRED says it is still comparing with the pair it first tested.
