MGA Miniverse Animals Series 3

A current Miniverse capsule on the official shop, in stock at $8.99. The line is already known. This series is the buyable drop, not a sold-out rumor.

Where to buy

Identified on October 28, 2026 (2026-10-28)

Signals are not guarantees. A sell-out, a search spike or a celebrity sighting can fade. This page describes public reporting. It is not a forecast, and it is not a reason to buy anything as an investment.

Early–mid

What it is

MGA’s Miniverse Make It Mini Animals Series 3 is a blind capsule for ages 8 and up. The official shop lists eight styles — corgi, dachshund, chihuahua, two kitten duos, raccoon, sloth, and otter — each with pieces for a tiny habitat. The price on that page is $8.99, and the variant we checked was available to add to cart.

Signals we can actually cite

  • Retail: the MGA shop files this series under Miniverse bestsellers and marks the listing new. That is the brand’s own merchandising, not a third-party sales audit.
  • What we are not counting: other Miniverse capsules show up in TikTok Shop toy rankings. One of those listings is an adults-only drink set, so it is not evidence for this animals series, and it is not a product we are pointing anyone toward.

No resale premium and no sell-through number for Series 3 is in the sources. “Early–mid” means a new series inside a line people already collect, still available at the list price.

Why this stage and meter

The meter stays at 2/5. We have a live product page and the brand’s bestseller shelf. We do not have an independent search spike, a cited post count, or a resale multiple for this capsule.

signal 2/5
