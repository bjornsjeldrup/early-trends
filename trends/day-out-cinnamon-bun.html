Day Out Cinnamon Bun protein balls

A cinnamon-bun protein ball from a small snack brand, in stock at $27.99. The TikTok Shop line is the company’s own post, not a sales audit. This is a snack, not a health promise.

Where to buy

Identified on October 28, 2026 (2026-10-28)

Signals are not guarantees. A sell-out, a search spike or a celebrity sighting can fade. This page describes public reporting. It is not a forecast, and it is not a reason to buy anything as an investment.

Early

What it is

Day Out sells cinnamon bun protein balls. The brand product page we opened was in stock at $27.99. The bag in the brand photo is marked cinnamon bun and “12g protein per serving.” That is a package label. It is not a claim that the snack does anything for your health.

Target also has a cinnamon bun multipack page. The button here goes to the brand page whose price and stock we could read directly.

Signals we can actually cite

  • Short-video commerce, from the brand: on September 17, 2026 Day Out posted that it was opening on TikTok Shop with a variety 4-pack and a cinnamon bun 4-pack, and that it was about to test which products shoppers picked.
  • Retail: the cinnamon bun page on getdayout.com was available when we checked, and Target has a separate multipack listing.

The LinkedIn post is the company talking about its own launch. It is not an independent unit count.

Why this stage and meter

Early, meter 2/5. A brand launch post plus a live shop page. No search chart and no retailer rank we measured ourselves.

signal 2/5
