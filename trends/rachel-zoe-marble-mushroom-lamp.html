Rachel Zoe marble mushroom lamp

A marble mushroom lamp that already had its viral moment in November 2025, reported back at HomeGoods in October 2026 for about $80. Mid, and a second wave — not a first sighting.

Published October 28, 2026

Signals are not guarantees. A sell-out, a search spike or a celebrity sighting can fade. This page describes public reporting. It is not a forecast, and it is not a reason to buy anything as an investment.

Mid

What it is

The Rachel Zoe marble mushroom lamp is a small stone-look table lamp that HomeGoods shoppers chased in late 2025. The price level in that coverage is about $80. In October 2026 it was reported back on the floor.

Signals we can actually cite

  • First wave: Hunker’s November 2025 piece documents the viral HomeGoods run. We are not re-describing that month as if it were happening again.
  • Second wave: October 2026 coverage says the lamp is back. A return is a different signal from a debut: some people already know the object, and the retailer has chosen to stock it again.

We do not have a figure for how fast this restock is moving. “Back” is not the same sentence as “sold out again.”

Why this stage and meter

This is a second wave, so the meter is not “how new is the lamp.” A November 2025 viral run is on the record, and October 2026 coverage says it is back at HomeGoods around $80. That is a past short-video wave plus a return to a mass retailer. We do not have a current sell-through rate, so the third point is the second-wave reading itself: it left and came back, which is momentum you can see without inventing a unit count. Illustrative meter: 3/5. Stage: mid.
