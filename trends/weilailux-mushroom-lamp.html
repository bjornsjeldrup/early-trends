WEILAILUX cordless mushroom lamp

A cordless mushroom lamp with a real Amazon page. The marble HomeGoods lamp in the press is a different object, and Mushi’s silicone light was not on Amazon.

Where to buy

Identified on October 28, 2026 (2026-10-28)

Signals are not guarantees. A sell-out, a search spike or a celebrity sighting can fade. This page describes public reporting. It is not a forecast, and it is not a reason to buy anything as an investment.

Mid

What it is

The Amazon listing is a WEILAILUX cordless mushroom table lamp, rechargeable, touch-dimmable, in a khaki or cream finish. The photo is that lamp, lit. It is not the Rachel Zoe marble lamp, and it is not the Mushi silicone light. Mushi had no Amazon page, so it is off the list.

Signals we can actually cite

  • The shape, in the press: Hunker and House Digest wrote up a marble mushroom lamp that HomeGoods shoppers chased. Those stories are not about WEILAILUX. They are why the shape is on this site.
  • A checkout: this brand’s lamp has an Amazon product page. We do not have a sell-out figure for it.

Why this stage and meter

Mid, meter 2/5. The mushroom-lamp look already had a retail moment. This is a named lamp in that shape that you can order on Amazon.com.
