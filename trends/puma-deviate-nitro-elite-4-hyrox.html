PUMA Deviate Nitro Elite 4 HYROX

A racing shoe built for HYROX, in a year when search interest in the sport is booming and a Korea sell-out has been reported. Mid — and the sell-out we can cite is one market.

Identified on October 28, 2026 (2026-10-28)

Signals are not guarantees. A sell-out, a search spike or a celebrity sighting can fade. This page describes public reporting. It is not a forecast, and it is not a reason to buy anything as an investment.

Mid

What it is

The Deviate Nitro Elite 4 HYROX is PUMA’s racing shoe for HYROX, the mixed fitness race that has been pulling in new participants. The trend is the sport; the pick is this shoe, because a named product with a reported sell-out is easier to track than “HYROX” as a feeling.

Signals we can actually cite

  • Search: a 2026 fitness-trends roundup describes a HYROX search boom. We are not reprinting a percentage the roundup’s headline does not hand us as a shoe statistic.
  • Sell-out, one market: Maeil Business reports a sell-out in Korea. That is the sell-out on the record. It is not a claim about US or European stock.

Why this stage and meter

Two cited signals: a HYROX search boom in a 2026 fitness-trends roundup, and a Korea sell-out reported by Maeil Business. We are not stretching the Korea report into a worldwide stock-out, and we are not adding a third point for vibe. Illustrative meter: 2/5. The stage is still “Mid” because the shoe is a branded performance model for a sport that already has a search footprint, not a garage prototype.
