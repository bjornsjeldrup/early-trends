What it is

Emotional Support Pals x NFL is a Relatable line of officially licensed plush footballs, an Amazon exclusive, across 12 teams. Each set is five removable plush footballs in a plush field basket. The September 3, 2026 press release lists the 49ers, Bears, Cowboys, Lions, Packers, Chiefs, Raiders, Patriots, Eagles, Steelers, Bills, and Broncos. The parent Emotional Support Pals brand was already a plush line. This page is about the new NFL sets. See current price on Amazon.

Emotional support football

An emotional support football here is a soft team-colored plush, not a ball you kick. The Toy Book describes five plush footballs tucked in a field basket, and its product facts list the age as 0 and up.

NFL plush football

The photo is Relatable’s Cowboys nfl plush football set, via The Toy Book. It is one of the 12. Where to buy opens the Amazon listing for the line. See current price on Amazon.

NFL stocking stuffers

Hip2Save, on September 15, 2026, wrote that the sets were available to preorder and were scheduled to release on November 1. A five-plush basket is a small fan gift, which is why it is on our stocking-stuffer guide as an nfl stocking stuffer. See current price on Amazon.

Gifts for football fans

The press release frames the line as gifts for football fans, including a little comfort when the game does not go their way. It is on the teens guide, the older-recipient list. It is not on the kids guide.

Emotional support plush

Emotional support plush is the parent idea. Relatable’s September 3 release says the brand had already grown from its original plush collection into food, entertainment, and now sports. We are not calling the whole brand new. The early label is for the NFL line.
