The Unselected Journals of Emma M. Lion

A self-published historical series that a major publisher bought through volume 8, with a ninth book signed. The BookTok citation is real and weak.

Identified on October 28, 2026 (2026-10-28)

Signals are not guarantees. A sell-out, a search spike or a celebrity sighting can fade. This page describes public reporting. It is not a forecast, and it is not a reason to buy anything as an investment.

Early

What it is

The Unselected Journals of Emma M. Lion is Beth Brower’s historical series, written as the diaries of a young woman in a fictional Victorian London neighborhood. Readers found it while it was self-published. Bloomsbury then bought volumes 1–8 and later signed the ninth book.

Signals we can actually cite

  • From self-published to a major list: The Bookseller reported Bloomsbury taking the series in an exclusive submission, covering the eight books already written.
  • The series continues: a later Bookseller note says volume 9 is signed, which matters because a pickup that stops at the backlist is a different story from one that keeps going.
  • BookTok, labeled weak: a shop blog argues that “everyone on BookTok” is obsessed. We are not treating a single store’s blog as evidence of a platform-wide surge. It is a mention, and a soft one.

Why this stage and meter

The solid signal is publishing traction: Bloomsbury acquired volumes 1–8 in an exclusive submission after the books had already found readers as self-published titles, then signed volume 9. A shop blog says BookTok is “suddenly obsessed.” That is one retailer’s post, not a platform metric, so it does not get a full short-video point. Illustrative meter: 2/5. No sales or library-hold figures are cited.
