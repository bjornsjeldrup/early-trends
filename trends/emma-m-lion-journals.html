What it is

The Unselected Journals of Emma M. Lion is Beth Brower’s historical series, written as the diaries of a young woman in a fictional Victorian London neighborhood. Readers found it while it was self-published. Bloomsbury then bought volumes 1–8 and later signed the ninth book.

Signals we can actually cite

  • From self-published to a major list: The Bookseller reported Bloomsbury taking the series in an exclusive submission, covering the eight books already written.
  • The series continues: a later Bookseller note says volume 9 is signed, which matters because a pickup that stops at the backlist is a different story from one that keeps going.
  • BookTok mention: a shop blog argues that “everyone on BookTok” is obsessed. That is one store’s blog, not a platform-wide count.

Why this stage and meter

The solid public fact is publishing traction: Bloomsbury acquired volumes 1–8 in an exclusive submission after the books had already found readers as self-published titles, then signed volume 9. A shop blog says BookTok is “suddenly obsessed.” That is one retailer’s post, not a platform metric. Meter: 2/5. No sales or library-hold figures are cited.
