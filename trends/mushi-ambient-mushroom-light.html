Mushi Ambient Mushroom Light

A $30 silicone mushroom light you can order. The marble HomeGoods lamp in the press still has no stable online product page, so that pick is gone. These are not the same object.

Where to buy

Identified on October 28, 2026 (2026-10-28)

Signals are not guarantees. A sell-out, a search spike or a celebrity sighting can fade. This page describes public reporting. It is not a forecast, and it is not a reason to buy anything as an investment.

Early

What it is

Mushi is a small silicone mushroom light from World of Mushi, listed at $30. The variant we checked was available, in orange, blue, pink, and red. The photo is the brand’s orange lamp. It is soft-looking and cartoon-faced. It is not stone, and it is not the Rachel Zoe marble lamp.

That marble lamp is off the site. Hunker and House Digest described a HomeGoods piece people hunted in stores. We never found a stable product URL for it. If it cannot be bought from a link, it does not stay.

Signals we can actually cite

  • The shape, in the press: Hunker’s November 2025 piece and House Digest’s follow-up are about the marble HomeGoods lamp, including a reported return to shelves around $80. Those articles are not about Mushi.
  • A lamp you can buy: the Mushi product page, in stock at $30. We have no sell-out, search, or resale figure for Mushi itself, and we are not inventing one.

Why this stage and meter

Early for this product, meter 2/5. The mushroom-lamp shape already had a retail moment. This write-up is only willing to say that a different lamp in that shape has a checkout. It does not say Mushi sold out at HomeGoods.

signal 2/5
