Vaseline Originals Brow Tamer Jelly

A petroleum-jelly spin on old beauty hacks that is already selling through on TikTok Shop in Thailand. For the US, it is still ahead of launch — very early.

Identified on October 28, 2026 (2026-10-28)

Signals are not guarantees. A sell-out, a search spike or a celebrity sighting can fade. This page describes public reporting. It is not a forecast, and it is not a reason to buy anything as an investment.

Very early

What it is

Vaseline Originals is a line that turns long-running creator hacks — jelly on brows, jelly as a primer or highlight — into named products. The two we are watching are Brow Tamer Jelly and the All-In-One Primer & Highlighter.

Signals we can actually cite

  • Seed: the line is explicitly built on beauty hacks creators were posting as far back as 2008, not on a new invention.
  • Short video and sell-outs: Unilever describes TikTok Live sell-outs in Thailand. Glossy reports one product every two seconds in the first month, expansion through Southeast Asia, and a US launch still planned.

We are not saying the jelly performs better than any other product, and we are not treating a Thailand sell-out as US demand. “Very early” is about where a US reader would be meeting it.

Why this stage and meter

Seed (creator hacks from 2008 turned into a product line), short video (TikTok Live and TikTok Shop), and sell-outs (including a reported rate of one product every two seconds in the first month) are all cited. Search-trend charts are not, so this is 3/5. The stage is still “Very early” because the heat described is in Thailand and Southeast Asia, and a US launch is described as planned rather than done. The meter is not a claim that the product works.
