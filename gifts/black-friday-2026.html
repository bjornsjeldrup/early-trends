  • Order early. The interesting gifts are the ones that disappear the week everyone else starts shopping.
  • Check stock when you click through to Amazon. A listing can be in stock in the morning and gone by dinner, and we do not reprint a live inventory number here.

Questions

Why is there no percent off?

A hand-typed discount goes stale, and Amazon's associate rules do not want publishers inventing an offer. The listing is the offer.

Are early picks mixed with popular ones?

Yes. The badge says which is which. A popular pick is a mainstream label on our page. It is not an early call.

When should I look?

Deals move. Check the Amazon page again closer to the day you buy, including the morning you intend to check out.
