Trend of the Week: BubblePlush Yoga Ball Buddies

hand2mind’s BubblePlush Yoga Ball Buddies are plush characters that slip over a small yoga ball. They sold out on TikTok Shop and are just starting their first chain-store rollout. First clear public signal: about two months ago.

Identified on October 28, 2026 (2026-10-28) · Trend age: about 2 months

Early Baby & Kids Mainstream filter: passed

signal 3/5

Where to buy

Signals are not guarantees. A sell-out can fade. This page describes public reporting. It is not a forecast, and it is not a reason to buy anything as an investment.

New this week: every Trend of the week must pass our mainstream filter and have a first clear signal under three months old. Last week’s NeeDoh pick failed that test; read why.

Why it passes

  • Trend age about 2 months: the first clear public signal is hand2mind’s September 2, 2026 announcement that the originals sold out on TikTok Shop in under two weeks.
  • Growth, not peak: three new characters, Amazon availability, and a first rollout to 222 Meijer stores in October.
  • Not mainstream yet: no national news features we could find, no Aldi, Walmart, Target or Costco exclusive, and a small TikTok footprint so far.
  • Wholesome: screen-free movement play with yoga pose cards for kids.

First-signal timeline

  • Early 2026: listed among hand2mind’s 2026 new products.
  • September 2, 2026: TikTok Shop sell-out, new characters and Meijer plan announced (PR Newswire).
  • October 2026: Meijer rollout begins.
  • Follow-up: we review this pick in February and May 2027.
