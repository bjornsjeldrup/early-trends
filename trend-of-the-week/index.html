Trend of the Week: Saie SuperSuede Baked Eyeshadow

Saie’s baked eyeshadow singles launched August 18, 2026. Bellini, a rose-gold shimmer co-created with Hannah Chody, sold out within hours on October 15. Label: Early. See current price on Amazon.

Identified on November 10, 2026 (2026-11-10) · First dated signal: August 18, 2026

Early Beauty & Makeup

signal 3/5

Where to buy

Signals are not guarantees. A sell-out can fade. This page describes public reporting. It is not a forecast, and it is not a reason to buy anything as an investment.

NeeDoh is a pick we published too late. Read why we relabelled it.

Why it is this week’s pick

  • Dated from the launch: August 18, 2026, the line launch, which is before the October 15 Bellini sell-out. Label: Early. About 2.8 months had passed by November 10, 2026.
  • A dated sell-out: Bellini, the rose-gold shimmer Hannah Chody co-created, sold out within hours on October 15. Allure says Sephora confirmed Saie was among the top-searched brands on its site that day.
  • Restocks, then a gap: trackalacker shows Bellini flipping in and out of stock at Saie from October 21 to October 27, and out of stock since that morning. Saie’s page still listed Bellini unavailable on November 10.
  • Where it sits in shops: the Amazon listing is new in the past month, with 6 ratings, around #14,094 in Beauty & Personal Care. A TikTok Shop gift set reportedly set records; the brand did not confirm that, and Allure says TikTok declined to comment.

First-signal timeline

  • August 18, 2026: SuperSuede Baked Eyeshadow launches, 11 shades, Sephora and Saie (WWD, Saie waitlist note). See current price on Amazon.
  • October 15, 2026: Bellini sells out within hours. Saie is among the most-searched brands on Sephora that day (Cosmetics Daily, Glasswend, Allure).
  • October 21–27, 2026: in-and-out stock at Saie, then out of stock (trackalacker).
  • Where to buy: Amazon only, and the link is the line listing. Bellini itself sells via Saie and Sephora and may not be on Amazon yet. The shade menu we opened did not include Bellini.
