Product Trend Forecasts Can Miss

Spotting a trend early is a hobby, not a science. Plenty of promising signals fizzle out, and that’s fine. We share what we see, explain why, and never promise that anything will “blow up” — or that buying it is a good idea. Please don’t treat products or collectibles as investments.
