What early signals look like here

These are the public sources we check first for pet supplies. One signal alone is noise; several lining up over a few weeks is worth a write-up.

Signal / sourceWhat we look for
Pet creator accountsPopular pet accounts on TikTok and Instagram featuring the same toy, bed or feeder.
Reddit pet communitiesCat, dog and small-pet subreddits posting the same product in “best purchase” threads.
Amazon Movers & ShakersFast climbers in Pet Supplies.
Pet retailer bestsellersNew items rising in bestseller lists at major pet retailers.
Google TrendsBreakouts for a product type plus “for cats” or “for dogs”.

Watch out: Every pet is different. Check materials, sizing and supervision guidance, and ask your vet about anything food- or health-related.
