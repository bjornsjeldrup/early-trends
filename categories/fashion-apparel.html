What early signals look like here

These are the public sources we check first for fashion & apparel. One signal alone is noise; several lining up over a few weeks is worth a write-up.

Signal / sourceWhat we look for
Street style & celebrity off-duty looksThe same item or silhouette spotted on several well-known people in casual, unsponsored settings (airport, street, concerts).
TikTok & Reels outfit formats“Outfit check”, “get ready with me” and styling videos featuring the same piece across many creators.
Pinterest TrendsSteadily rising saves and searches for an aesthetic or item name, especially ahead of a season.
Resale sell-throughItems selling quickly on Depop, Vinted, Poshmark or eBay, and new listings using the trend name in titles.
Google TrendsBreakout searches for an aesthetic label (often ending in “-core”) or a specific item style.
Amazon Movers & ShakersFast climbers in Clothing, Shoes & Jewelry — often affordable versions of the look.

Watch out: Fast fashion copies trends quickly. When possible we point to durable, well-reviewed options rather than throwaway buys.
