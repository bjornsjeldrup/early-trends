What early signals look like here

These are the public sources we check first for kitchen & dining. One signal alone is noise; several lining up over a few weeks is worth a write-up.

Signal / sourceWhat we look for
TikTok recipe videosA recipe trend that relies on a particular tool or pan, repeated across many creators.
Amazon Movers & ShakersFast climbers in Kitchen & Dining.
Pinterest TrendsRising saves for a tableware style or kitchen organization idea.
Reddit cooking communitiesCooking and meal-prep subreddits recommending the same gadget.
KickstarterKitchen gadget campaigns funding quickly.
Google TrendsBreakouts for a gadget name or “[recipe] without [tool]” searches.

Watch out: Single-use gadgets pile up fast. We say when a trend is genuinely useful and when a pan you already own will do.
