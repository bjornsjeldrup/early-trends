Books & Media

Books and media often rise slowly and then all at once: a reader community champions a title, library holds pile up, and it lands on bestseller lists long after the first fans found it.

What early signals look like here

These are the public sources we check first for books & media. One signal alone is noise; several lining up over a few weeks is worth a write-up.

Signal / sourceWhat we look for
BookTok & BookstagramEmotional reaction videos, annotation tours and “books that made me cry” formats featuring the same title.
Goodreads & StoryGraph“Want to read” shelf counts and early reviews growing quickly before release or in the weeks after.
Amazon Best Sellers rankA title climbing Best Sellers or Movers & Shakers in Books, especially in a niche subcategory first.
Library hold queuesLong hold lists for a title on library apps such as Libby.
KickstarterBoard games, comics and art books funding far beyond their goal in the first days.
Google TrendsBreakouts for an author, series or game name.

Watch out: We stick to all-ages and general-audience titles and note content warnings where relevant.

The general method is explained in How we spot trends.
