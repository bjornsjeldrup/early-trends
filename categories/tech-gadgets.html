Tech & Gadgets

Tech trends are often visible before launch: on crowdfunding platforms and product-launch sites. The challenge is telling real momentum from marketing.

What early signals look like here

These are the public sources we check first for tech & gadgets. One signal alone is noise; several lining up over a few weeks is worth a write-up.

Signal / sourceWhat we look for
Product Hunt launchesHardware and accessory launches with strong organic upvotes and active, specific comments.
Kickstarter & Indiegogo paceCampaigns hitting their goal in hours or days, with many small backers rather than a few large ones.
“Gadgets you need” videosTikTok and YouTube Shorts gadget roundups featuring the same item across unrelated channels.
Reddit gadget communitiesEnthusiast subreddits sharing setups and asking where to buy a specific accessory.
Amazon Movers & ShakersFast climbers in Electronics or Computers & Accessories.
Google TrendsBreakouts for a product type (e.g. a new kind of charger or desk accessory).

Watch out: Crowdfunded products can ship late or not at all. We flag crowdfunding risk clearly and prefer products you can buy today.

The general method is explained in How we spot trends.
