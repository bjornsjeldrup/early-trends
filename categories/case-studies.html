What early signals look like here

A case study is a backtest. We ask which detector would have lit first, using only sources we can link. One signal is a story. Two that agree are a lesson.

Signal / sourceWhat we look for
Specialty sell-out and restockA first batch or a restock gone, a shop queue, a purchase limit. Often before a celebrity post.
Crowdfunding, plus a second signalA blowout on Kickstarter or a sibling site. A pledge total alone does not count. We want a ship, a reorder, or a real retailer afterwards.
Drop-miss communitiesPeople writing that they missed the drop, not only that they liked the ad.
Five-second wowA clip that explains the trick in one breath. A format score. Not demand by itself.
The late orderCelebrity, then a TikTok compilation, then Google Trends, then resale. Useful as a contrast. Usually late.

Watch out: Hindsight makes every sell-out look obvious. These pages are lessons. They are not a scoreboard of calls we made.
