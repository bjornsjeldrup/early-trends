Toys & Collectibles

Collectibles are where viral crazes are easiest to see: a character catches on, a series sells out, and suddenly everyone has one clipped to their bag. Labubu is the textbook recent example.

What early signals look like here

These are the public sources we check first for toys & collectibles. One signal alone is noise; several lining up over a few weeks is worth a write-up.

Signal / sourceWhat we look for
Celebrity & fandom sightingsA pop star, athlete or streamer seen with a figure or bag charm — organically, not as an ad — and fans asking “what is that?”
TikTok unboxingsBlind-box opening videos and “collection tour” posts repeating across many unrelated accounts.
Collector communitiesReddit collector subreddits and public Discord or forum threads filling up with trade, hunt and restock posts.
Resale sold listingsSold (not asking) prices on eBay and other marketplaces rising above retail for common figures, not just rare “secret” ones.
Amazon Movers & ShakersA toy or series jumping sharply in the Toys & Games Movers & Shakers list or climbing Best Sellers.
Google TrendsBreakout related queries like “[name] restock” or “where to buy [name]”.

Watch out: Collectible hype can be driven by artificial scarcity. We cover the fun, not flipping — please don’t buy toys as investments.
