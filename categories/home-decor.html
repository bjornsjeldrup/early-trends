Home & Decor

Home trends move with aesthetics: a look gets a name, room-makeover videos spread it, and specific lamps, rugs and mirrors start selling out.

What early signals look like here

These are the public sources we check first for home & decor. One signal alone is noise; several lining up over a few weeks is worth a write-up.

Signal / sourceWhat we look for
Pinterest TrendsRising saves and searches for a decor style or item, often months ahead of retail.
Room makeover videosTikTok and Reels makeovers and “rental-friendly upgrade” videos repeating the same piece.
Etsy bestsellers & trendingHandmade versions of an item multiplying, with “bestseller” badges on new listings.
Reddit home communitiesDecor, apartment and DIY subreddits sharing the same style or DIY hack.
Amazon Movers & ShakersFast climbers in Home & Kitchen > Home Décor.
Google TrendsBreakouts for a style name or “[item] DIY”.

Watch out: Check sizing and materials before buying viral decor — photos often flatter.

The general method is explained in How we spot trends.
