Health & Wellness

Wellness trends often start as a habit (a walking routine, a sleep ritual) and then attach to a piece of gear. We cover the gear and the habit — never medical treatments, supplements or weight-loss products.

What early signals look like here

These are the public sources we check first for health & wellness. One signal alone is noise; several lining up over a few weeks is worth a write-up.

Signal / sourceWhat we look for
Fitness & routine creators“Morning routine”, “desk workout” and “sleep routine” videos featuring the same accessory.
Google TrendsBreakouts for a habit or gear name (for example, a new type of walking or stretching accessory).
Reddit communitiesFitness, sleep and productivity subreddits recommending a specific tool.
Amazon Movers & ShakersFast climbers in Health & Household or Sports & Fitness equipment.
Product Hunt & KickstarterNew wellness gadgets (timers, lamps, trackers) gaining quick traction.

Watch out: Nothing here is medical advice. We don’t cover supplements, diet pills or health claims — talk to a professional about your health.
