What early signals look like here

These are the public sources we check first for food & drink. One signal alone is noise; several lining up over a few weeks is worth a write-up.

Signal / sourceWhat we look for
TikTok taste tests & recipes“Trying the viral…” videos and recipe remixes repeating across many creators.
International snack importsProducts from Asian, Latin American or European grocery imports showing up at mainstream stores or snack-box services.
Google Trends flavor breakoutsBreakout searches for a flavor or dish (often paired with “recipe” or “where to buy”).
Reddit food communitiesSnack-hunting and grocery subreddits posting finds, sightings and restock tips.
Limited-edition sell-outsSeasonal or limited flavors selling out quickly at chains and grocery stores.
Amazon Grocery moversFast climbers in Grocery & Gourmet Food Movers & Shakers.

Watch out: Always check allergen labels on imported snacks. No alcohol, energy-drink hype or diet-pill claims here.
